Enterprise deployments may include:

* Dedicated Litecoin nodes
* Custom rate limits
* Private networking
* On-premise or hybrid setups
* Custom SLAs

Operational controls can include:

* IP allowlists
* Reverse proxies
* Custom monitoring hooks

These controls increase **isolation and capacity**, not trust.
